Vision · §1

"I am the agent. I close the loop."

  • For keyboard players who know, or are learning, what a coding agent does.
  • Delivering the feeling of finishing ordered tasks under hostile, ambiguous conditions.
  • Through one honest jump, ordered task stations, instant retry with a named cause.
  • Between the First Steps slice and a Celeste-style readable-death platformer.

ASSUMPTION audience narrowed from the starter's "short readable challenge".

What I would have said out loud

  • "Task A, B until done" as stations is a checklist. A loop has a verify step, and verify is where agents fail. Left out on purpose; first thing to add if the ledger feels flat.
  • Malware undo is the only mechanic that makes the theme mechanical. Build it first, in a greybox, watch one person.
  • The vague path is fair only if the wrong branch is boring. One hazard on it and testers will say the sign lied.
  • First fix: answer Q-01 and Q-04. Everything else waits for a greybox.
